\section{Supplementary formal verification}\label{app:formalization}

The mathematical arguments in the preceding sections are self-contained and do
not depend on formal proof software.  A separate Lean 4 development accompanies
the paper as supplementary verification of the definitions and theorem
statements.

The repository uses Lean~4.33.0 and contains machine-checked counterparts of
the free-completion construction, finite-family separation theorem, completion
results, arithmetic examples, and the comparison with the many-sorted
partial-to-total encoding.  The command
\begin{lstlisting}[style=lean]
bash scripts/verify.sh
\end{lstlisting}
rebuilds the formal development and checks that the public theorem files contain
no proof holes.

The formalization is provided for reproducibility and error checking only.  It
is not used as a substitute for the mathematical proofs in the paper, and no
claim of mathematical significance or priority is based on the existence of the
Lean development.  The exact correspondence between numbered results in the
paper and formal declarations is recorded separately in the repository's
\texttt{THEOREM\_MAP.md} file.
