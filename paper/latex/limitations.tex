\section{Limitations}\label{sec:limitations}

The results proved here are deliberately restricted.

First, the main theorem is stated for one-sorted algebras with a finite binary
signature.  The finite-subterm construction suggests broader finitary versions,
but these are not needed for the present paper.

Second, the bound obtained from the construction is not asserted to be optimal.
For a finite set $X$, the proof uses at most $|N(X)|+1$ new elements.  Determining
minimal complement size for particular signatures or classes of partial
algebras is a separate problem.

Third, the finite-base properness theorem is an exact criterion, but the
base-fixing-context theorem for infinite bases is only sufficient.  No complete
classification of properness over arbitrary infinite carriers is claimed.

Fourth, the completion theorem concerns universal equations only insofar as the
corresponding continuity argument is explicitly proved.  No general statement
about conditional equations or arbitrary first-order theories is intended.

Finally, the machine-checked development accompanying the paper does not replace
any proof in the text and is not part of the novelty claim.  Its role is limited
to supplementary verification of the stated formal results.
