\section{Supplementary formal verification}\label{app:formalization}

The mathematical arguments in the preceding sections are independent of the
machine-checked development.  The formalization is provided only as an
additional consistency check on the stated definitions and proofs.

The repository contains Lean sources corresponding to the free-completion,
finite-subset discrimination, completion, properness, and equation-preservation
results.  A theorem-to-source map and a reproducible verification script are
included with the source package.  The formal development is not used to
establish novelty, significance, or historical priority.

As with any formalization, the verification claim is conditional on the
correctness of the formal statement, the proof assistant kernel, and the
imported libraries.  The paper therefore asks the reader to assess the
mathematical proofs above on their own terms.
