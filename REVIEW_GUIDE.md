manuscript architecture. They no longer describe the paper on this branch and
are retained in Git history only.

For review of the current work, use:

- `paper/latex/paper.tex` and its six included section files;
- `README.md` for scope and status;
- `scripts/check_manuscript.py` for static manuscript/repository consistency;
- `scripts/build-paper.sh` for the deterministic PDF build;
- `lean/` as supplementary formal development, not as a claim that every
  sentence of the current manuscript has a declaration with identical wording.

The current manuscript is **A Finite-Complement Congruence Topology for Partial
Algebras**.
